How SeatSwap works

Many plans, like team, family or group plans, include several seats, but one person pays for all of them. SeatSwap connects that person with people who want a seat.

1

A host lists an eligible subscription

They already pay for a plan with room for more people. They set a price per seat and how many seats are open.

2

You choose an available seat

Browse groups for the service you want, check the host and the price, and pick an open seat.

3

You pay your share and use it

The plan’s cost is split between the people in the group, so each person pays far less than the full price. In this prototype, joining only reserves a seat — no payment is taken yet.

Clear by design

No hidden mechanics and no inflated promises. What a card shows is what the server enforces — and we say plainly what is not built yet.

Clear pricing

Every group lists one price per seat per month, set by the host. SeatSwap’s own fees are not decided yet, so nothing is added on top.

Real seat availability

When you join, the server checks the seat is still free and locks it in one step. Two people can never take the same seat.

Structured booking

Pick a seat, reserve it, and it is held for you as payment pending. Payments are not live yet, so no money moves when you join.

Honest about what’s missing

Host and subscription verification are planned, not built. Until then nobody gets a verified badge — and nobody here claims one.
